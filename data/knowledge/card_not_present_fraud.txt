PLAYBOOK: Card-Not-Present (CNP) Fraud

SIGNALS
- Transaction submitted without physical card presence (e-commerce, MOTO).
- Rule engine fires with reason "Card not present".
- High model risk score on CNP transactions with new merchants.

RISK FACTORS
- CNP transaction to a new or unverified merchant.
- Billing address does not match registered address (AVS mismatch).
- IP address originates from a country different from the billing address.
- Transaction amount close to the card limit or above historical average.

MITIGATING FACTORS
- Merchant is known and has been used previously in CNP mode.
- Transaction amount is consistent with user's CNP spending history.
- AVS check passed; IP matches registered country.

INVESTIGATION STEPS
1. Verify whether the merchant has been used in prior CNP transactions.
2. Check AVS and CVV verification results if available.
3. Inspect IP geolocation against billing/shipping address.
4. Review whether the transaction amount exceeds the user's typical CNP spend.

RECOMMENDATION CRITERIA
- CONFIRM_FRAUD if: CNP + new merchant + foreign IP + AVS mismatch + high amount.
- ESCALATE if: CNP + new merchant with insufficient contextual data.
- FALSE_POSITIVE if: CNP to a known merchant with clean IP and AVS pass.

POLICY REFERENCES
- Fraud Policy FP-005: All CNP transactions above $500 to new merchants require manual review.
- SOP-CNP-01: CNP fraud evidence must be documented before initiating a chargeback.
