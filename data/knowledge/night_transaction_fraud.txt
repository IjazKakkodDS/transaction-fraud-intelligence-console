PLAYBOOK: Night-Time Transaction Fraud

SIGNALS
- Transaction timestamp between 00:00 and 05:59 UTC.
- Rule engine fires rule_flag=1 with reason "Night transaction".
- Elevated fraud base-rate during this window across all fraud typologies.

RISK FACTORS
- Transaction during hours when cardholders are typically asleep.
- Night transaction combined with high amount or new merchant.
- Geographic mismatch: transaction originates from a timezone where it is daytime
  while user's registered timezone indicates nighttime.
- Velocity spike during the same overnight window.

MITIGATING FACTORS
- User has a documented history of late-night transactions (shift worker, frequent traveller).
- Transaction amount is low and merchant is familiar.
- User's registered country operates in a timezone where the transaction falls within business hours.

INVESTIGATION STEPS
1. Review timestamp against user's historical transaction hour distribution.
2. Check whether night transactions are normal for this user segment.
3. Inspect IP geolocation to identify timezone mismatch.
4. Assess whether other fraud signals co-occur (amount, merchant, velocity).

RECOMMENDATION CRITERIA
- CONFIRM_FRAUD if: night transaction + high amount + new merchant + high risk score.
- ESCALATE if: night transaction is the only signal and user profile is unavailable.
- FALSE_POSITIVE if: user has consistent late-night spending pattern.

POLICY REFERENCES
- Fraud Policy FP-002: Automatic REVIEW flag for all out-of-hours transactions above $500.
- SOP-NIGHT-01: Night transaction investigations must complete within 4 business hours.
