PLAYBOOK: High-Amount Transaction Fraud

SIGNALS
- Transaction amount exceeds the high-value threshold (default $1,000).
- Rule engine fires rule_flag=1 with reason "High amount".
- Often co-occurs with night_transaction or new_merchant signals.

RISK FACTORS
- Single large transaction with no prior history of similar amounts.
- Amount significantly above the user's 30-day average.
- Amount near common money-mule round-number targets ($5,000, $10,000).
- Cross-border transaction combined with high amount.

MITIGATING FACTORS
- User has a historical pattern of large purchases (e.g., business account).
- Merchant is well-known and previously used by this user.
- Low model risk score despite high amount.

INVESTIGATION STEPS
1. Compare transaction amount against user's 30-day amount percentile.
2. Check whether the merchant has been seen before in user history.
3. Review IP geolocation against user's registered country.
4. Check for companion transactions (card-testing pattern: small tx followed by large tx).

RECOMMENDATION CRITERIA
- CONFIRM_FRAUD if: high amount + night transaction + new merchant + no prior history.
- ESCALATE if: high amount alone, user profile unavailable, or ambiguous context.
- FALSE_POSITIVE if: amount fits user's historical pattern and merchant is familiar.

POLICY REFERENCES
- Fraud Policy FP-001: Mandatory review for all transactions above $1,000.
- Fraud Policy FP-007: Escalation required when user history is unavailable.
