PLAYBOOK: Account Takeover (ATO)

SIGNALS
- Velocity spike: multiple transactions in a short window from the same account.
- Sudden change in transaction geography or device fingerprint.
- Model risk score elevated with reason "Model flagged" or "Suspicious IP".
- New merchant combined with high amount immediately after account login.

RISK FACTORS
- Transactions from an IP flagged in threat intelligence feeds.
- Rapid succession of transactions across multiple merchants in different regions.
- Password reset or account change event immediately preceding the transaction.
- Transaction pattern is entirely inconsistent with user's historical behaviour.

MITIGATING FACTORS
- Single transaction with no velocity pattern.
- Merchant and amount are consistent with user's prior behaviour.
- No geographic anomaly.

INVESTIGATION STEPS
1. Pull all transactions in the 24 hours preceding the flagged transaction.
2. Check IP address reputation and geographic location.
3. Review account login events for the same period (if accessible).
4. Assess whether multiple merchants or geographies appear in the velocity window.

RECOMMENDATION CRITERIA
- CONFIRM_FRAUD if: velocity spike + suspicious IP + new merchant + high amount.
- ESCALATE if: velocity pattern is present but context is ambiguous.
- FALSE_POSITIVE if: merchant and amount are consistent and IP is clean.

POLICY REFERENCES
- Fraud Policy FP-004: Immediate block required when velocity threshold exceeded and IP flagged.
- SOP-ATO-01: ATO cases must be escalated to the Fraud Operations team within 1 hour.
