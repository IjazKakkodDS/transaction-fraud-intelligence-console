PLAYBOOK: Velocity / Card-Testing Fraud

SIGNALS
- Multiple transactions submitted in a short time window from the same card or account.
- Pattern: one or more small test transactions immediately followed by a large transaction.
- Rule engine fires with reason "Velocity spike".
- Model flags the pattern after observing the burst in the feature window.

RISK FACTORS
- Three or more transactions within 10 minutes across different merchants.
- Small authorisation attempts ($0.01–$5.00) preceding a large transaction — classic card-testing.
- Transactions spread across merchants in multiple geographic regions within minutes.
- Multiple declined transactions followed by a successful one.

MITIGATING FACTORS
- Transactions occur at the same merchant (e.g., retried payment).
- User has a documented pattern of rapid sequential purchases (e.g., grocery and fuel on the same trip).
- All transactions are small and no large follow-on transaction is present.

INVESTIGATION STEPS
1. Pull all transactions in the 60-minute window surrounding the flagged transaction.
2. Identify whether small test transactions precede a large transaction.
3. Check merchant diversity: are multiple unrelated merchants involved?
4. Assess geographic spread of transaction origin IPs.

RECOMMENDATION CRITERIA
- CONFIRM_FRAUD if: velocity spike + card-testing pattern + large follow-on transaction + new merchants.
- ESCALATE if: velocity spike is present but card-testing pattern is ambiguous.
- FALSE_POSITIVE if: burst is explained by a single merchant or known user behaviour.

POLICY REFERENCES
- Fraud Policy FP-003: Accounts with velocity spike + decline pattern must be temporarily suspended pending review.
- SOP-VEL-01: Card-testing investigations require correlation with declined transaction logs.
